NusaStat — Indonesia in Numbers. An independent compilation of published national statistics. Figures are from Statistics Indonesia (BPS), the World Bank, Bank Indonesia, APJII and the Ministry of Health, each labelled with its source and year on the Data & sources page.

Not affiliated with BPS, the World Bank or any publisher named here. All figures as published for 2024 unless a figure says otherwise.
